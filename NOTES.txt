E-Commerce SQL Analysis - final query bundle

All queries use WHERE o.Status = 'Completed'.
Pending, Cancelled, and Returned orders are excluded.

Product profit = quantity * (unitPrice - unitCost).
Profit does not include other operating expenses.

Database tables and column names used:
- customers (CustomerID, CustomerName)
- orders (OrderID, CustomerID, OrderDate, Status, PaymentMethod)
- order_items (OrderID, ProductID, quantity, unitPrice)
- products (ProductID, ProductName, unitCost)

The SQL scripts were run and their outputs reviewed against the project results.
